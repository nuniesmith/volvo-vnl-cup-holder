# Photo review of the stock cup holder

Photos taken 2026-09-29 on an iPhone 17 Pro Max. The tape readings are approximate:
parallax and tape angle make them good to about ±0.1 in.

## Part markings (IMG_0155)

Molded on the underside of the body:

- **84752175 REV P02** (Volvo part number and revision)
- **8230-001VN**, **MA/2104P FR**, **10058762**, **63409/35787** (supplier and mold codes)
- **>PC/ABS<**, meaning the body is a polycarbonate/ABS blend. PETG or ASA are reasonable printed substitutes for cab heat; plain PLA may soften in a hot truck.

## Clone notes

- The clip and spring are a separate sub-assembly, so a two-part print (body + clip) matches the original.
- There are **two springs**: the compression spring under the orange clip (~27 mm, photo reading) and a
  **torsion spring** on a metal pin that loads the grey tongue. The tongue could instead be a printed flex
  finger (PETG flexes well), but a flex finger may take a set in cab heat.
- The ordered **25 mm** springs (a kit) are about 2 mm shorter than the stock compression spring.
  Model the clip's spring pocket and the body boss around the **new** spring's OD, wire size and
  free length, and check that the preload still returns the clip.
- The clip's pivot pins are molded in. On a printed clip, they can be printed in place (clip lying
  flat, pins horizontal) or replaced with a metal pin through a hole. Decide after measuring the pin diameter.
- The tulip cup walls, clip posts and flange need supports or a split print. The body's print orientation
  gets decided once the mount interface is measured.
